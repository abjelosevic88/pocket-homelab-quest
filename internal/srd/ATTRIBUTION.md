# Rules content

Pocket Quest includes rules content — monsters, spells, equipment, magic items,
classes, species, backgrounds, conditions and rules text — from the System
Reference Documents, compacted by `scripts/srd/convert.py` from the
[5e-bits/5e-database](https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database) (MIT) at commit
`a6212beb4b278917c2cff41c2b04c4ef4f3e0d6f`.

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1
(“SRD 5.1”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at
https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2
(“SRD 5.2”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at
https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2 is licensed under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

From SRD 5.2 Pocket Quest uses the encounter-building guidance (the XP Budget
per Character table and its Low / Moderate / High difficulties); everything
else is SRD 5.1. Pocket Quest is compatible with fifth edition (5E
compatible). The artwork in Pocket Quest (class emblems, dice, tokens) is its
own.
